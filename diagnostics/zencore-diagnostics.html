2. What to read

The slot address follows the editor: tone = 30 00 00 00 + (track × 17 + clip) × 02 00 00; drum kit = 32 10 00 00 + (track × 17 + clip) × 03 00 00; clip 17 is the track's own sound. On an MC-101 pick its own track number (1–4).

3. Compare two reads

Read, keep as A, change ONE thing on the MC-707, read again, keep as B, then compare.

Note: the MC-707 drops the top bit of reply addresses, so partials 1 and 2 (and 3 and 4) answer with the same address. Identical replies there are expected, not errors.
