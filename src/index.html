Live Mode

Space starts and stops. Keys 1-8 and Q-I trigger the 16 pads (two rows of 8, as on the MC-707). Scene pads, fills, swaps and mutes all wait for the launch point you choose. The key octave changes the key of all tone parts; the mute octave mutes parts.

Recording

Select a clip, press Rec in the editor and play. With a MIDI input chosen, pads and keys are recorded. Without one, use the computer keyboard: A S D F G H J K L are notes (W E T Y U O P are sharps; Z and X change octave). On drum patterns the keys A S D F G H J K L Q W E trigger the lanes. Live mode loops and overdubs; Step mode adds one note at a time (Space adds a rest). Esc stops.

Right-click

Almost everything has a right-click menu: clips, lanes, the ruler, track headers, library patterns, the editor grid, note rows and the velocity lane. In the editor, right-click an empty tone cell to stamp a chord, or a row to fill it or make a Euclidean rhythm.
